Ponnar - Sankar  
(Annanmar Varalaru)

Preface to the  
Eighth Edition

This novel, “Ponnar - Sankar,” which tells the history of the Annanmar, was published as a continuing serial in the weekly magazine “Kungumam.” Even then, its readership grew into the hundreds of thousands. This serial history was compiled as a book, and within just fifteen days of the first edition being released, all the copies were sold out. After that, seven editions were released one after another and all of them sold out; the eighth edition is now being published. The history of “Ponnar - Sankar” has also appeared as a screen epic and has run successfully throughout Tamil Nadu.

I convey my heartfelt thanks to Tamilkkani Publications, who hold the rights to the book “Ponnar - Sankar,” and to “Poompuhar” Publications, who are publishing the eighth edition. At the same time, I shower my appreciation and good wishes upon the Tamil people who have given this book such great support.

Date : 19-03-2015  
Chennai
