“Hey, bring water!” Chinnamalaik Kozhundhu shouted. A warrior ran up carrying a vessel of water. Chinnamalaik Kozhundhu laid the wounded warrior in his lap, sprinkled water on his face, and poured a little into his mouth as well.

“What happened, man? Where is Vaiyam Perumaan? What happened to Silambayi, Muthayi and Pavalayi? Are they coming?” he asked in agitation. The warrior shook his head as if to say no and shed tears.

“Just tell me what happened!” Chinnamalaik Kozhundhu's voice trembled as he asked.

Broken, shattered words spilled from the warrior's mouth.

“Thalaiyur Kaali... his commander Thirumalai... arrested Vaiyam Perumaan... everyone to Raachchaandaar Malai... to marry both girls to Maanthiyappan...”

With these words, the faithful warrior's life came to an end!

“What?” Chinnamalaik Kozhundhu roared as he sprang to his feet!
